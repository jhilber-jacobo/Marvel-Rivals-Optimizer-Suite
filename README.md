## 📦 Core Feature Matrix: What You're Actually Getting

### 🎯 Precision Calibration Module (PCM)
The heart of the suite. Unlike binary hit detection, PCM uses **continuous spatial deviation tracking**. It doesn't just tell you if you missed — it shows you *by how much*, *in which direction*, and *during which phase of your flick movement* the error occurred.

- **Spatial Error Heatmaps**: Visualize your aim pattern as a 3D surface
- **Temporal Offset Analysis**: Identifies timing inconsistencies in your shots
- **Crosshair Recovery Metrics**: Measures how quickly you reacquire targets after whiffing
- **Predictive Path Tracing**: Anticipates your next move and pre-validates it

### 🧠 Decision Velocity Trainer (DVT)
Aiming is only half the battle. DVT focuses on **rapid context-switching** — the ability to instantly alter your strategy when a team fight shifts unpredictably. This module presents you with **micro-scenarios** (1-3 second windows) and scores your response appropriateness.

- **2,500+ Unique Micro-Scenarios**: Compiled from actual high-tier match footage
- **Priority Assessment Scoring**: Grades your target selection logic
- **Resource Management Simulator**: Tracks your ultimate economy decisions
- **Pressure-State Graduation**: Slowly introduces time constraints to build composure

### 🔄 Reflex Pattern Harmonizer (RPH)
Your fingers have muscle memory you don't know about. RPH identifies **systematic micro-movements** that are wasting milliseconds. It then runs **targeted correction protocols** to rewire those patterns.

- **Micro-Tic Detection**: Flags involuntary mouse movements under 50ms
- **Stutter-Step Eliminator**: Fixes rhythm inconsistencies in your strafe patterns
- **Ability Sequence Optimizer**: Finds faster input orders for combo rotations
- **Sensitivity Fingerprint**: Maps your optimal DPI/aim-style combination

### 📊 Performance Analytics Dashboard (PAD)
Data without context is noise. PAD transforms your raw metrics into **coaching-grade insights**. It generates weekly trend reports, compares you against percentile benchmarks, and identifies your **yield-on-investment** for each training module.

- **Cross-Session Trend Lines**: See your improvement curves over months
- **Comparative Percentile Ranking**: Understand where you sit globally
- **Benchmark Decay Detection**: Alerts you if skills are regressing
- **Session Fatigue Index**: Tells you when your practice is counterproductive

## 💻 Technical Architecture: Built for Stability

The suite runs as a **native Windows application** with a lightweight overlay engine. It does not modify game files, inject code, or interfere with anti-cheat protocols. Instead, it operates purely as an **external analysis companion** — reading screen output and providing coaching feedback through an independent overlay.

### System Requirements
| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **OS** | Windows 10 (2004+) | Windows 11 22H2+ |
| **RAM** | 8 GB | 16 GB |
| **Storage** | 500 MB | 1 GB (for replay storage) |
| **Graphics** | DirectX 11 capable | DirectX 12 Ultra |
| **Display** | 1080p, 60Hz | 1440p+, 144Hz+ |

### Multilingual Support 🌍
The interface is translates across **12 languages** including English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, and Hindi. All coaching feedback text is localized.

### Responsive UI Design
The overlay resizes flawlessly from 720p to 4K resolutions. Windows are draggable, collapsible, and fully customizable for color-blind users. **Every metric can be pinned, hidden, or rearranged** to suit your focus.

## 📈 Installation Workflow

The installation process is designed to be **invisible to the user** — a single-step deployment that self-configures based on your system’s hardware profile. After running the installer:

1. The system detects your GPU and monitors refresh rate.
2. It calibrates its own polling frequency to match your hardware.
3. It creates a personalized training schedule based on your available playtime.
4. It scans for existing match replays to estimate your current skill baseline.

The entire process takes approximately **90 seconds** on a standard NVMe drive. No system changes are made outside the application’s own directory, and a **full uninstaller** is provided.
